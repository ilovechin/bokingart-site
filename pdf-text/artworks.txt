ABOUT BOKING LIVING


    当代生活中，我们对美的追求不仅限于艺术的领域，还延伸至我们的居住空间。在这个背景下，铂
晶生活诞生了。
     铂晶生活是铂晶艺术旗下的品牌，专注于打造独具匠心的艺术家居。我们的产品以铂晶元素为主题，
将艺术与生活完美融合，为您的居住环境带来无与伦比的优雅和璀璨。
铂晶生活的产品不仅仅是简单的家居用品，更是一种生活态度和品味的象征。我们相信，艺术的力量能
够提升生活的品质，让每一个细节都变得令人愉悦和感动 . 我们希望通过我们的作品，为您带来美的享
受和灵感，让您的生活充满艺术的力量。


In contemporary life, our pursuit of beauty is not limited to the field of art, but also extends to our
living spaces. In this context, BOKING LIVING was born.
BOKING LIVING is a brand under BOKING ART, focusing on creating unique artistic homes. Our
products are themed with artificial crystal elements, perfectly integrating art and life, bringing
unparalleled elegance and brilliance to your living environment.
BOKING LIVING's products are not just simple household items, but also a symbol of life attitude
and taste. We believe that the power of art can improve the quality of life and make every detail
pleasant and touching. We hope that through our works, we can bring you the enjoyment and
inspiration of beauty and fill your life with the power of art.

                                     The Beauty Of Statue

      古老的人类文明留下了无数优秀的造型文化，通过艺术家的灵感和创造力，以当代的审美观念

      The ancient human civilization has left countless excellent modeling cultures, through the
      inspiration and creativity of artists, with contemporary aesthetic conceptsAnd creative
      techniques, giving new life to traditional shapes.The solemn and solemn Buddha statue,
      with its long history and spiritual connotation, carries wisdom and faith, endowed by
      BOKING artistThe wonderful dialogue between ancient classic shapes and contemporary
      art makes us feel a kind of beauty beyond the material world.
