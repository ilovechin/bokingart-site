Lighting Direction

Art is the attitude. Light is the atmosphere.

The 2025 artistic lighting catalog presents Bokingart's crystal lighting as a bridge between sculpture and illumination. The main Endless Vitality direction uses botanical and leaf-inspired forms to bring movement, hope, and refined glow into interiors.

Product families include round chandeliers, wall lights, floor lamps, table lamps, golden branch forms, plum-blossom inspired pieces, and spiritual crystal pendant concepts for luxury residential and hospitality use.

  • Crystal chandeliers for foyers, dining rooms, galleries, and hotel public areas.
  • Wall lights, table lamps, and floor lamps for layered interior lighting plans.
  • Bespoke scale, cluster composition, and installation direction for project spaces.
