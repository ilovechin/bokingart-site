                                                   The Beauty Of Statue

     古老的人类文明留下了无数优秀的造型文化，通过艺术家的灵感和创造力，以当代的审美观念
     和创作手法，赋予传统造型新的生命。
     庄严肃穆的佛像造型，以其悠远的历史与精神内涵，承载着智慧与信仰，BOKING 艺术家赋予
     古老的经典造型与当代艺术之间的奇妙对话，使我们感受到一种超越物质世界的美好。



     The ancient human civilization has left countless excellent modeling cultures, through the
     inspiration and creativity of artists, with contemporary aesthetic conceptsAnd creative
     techniques, giving new life to traditional shapes.The solemn and solemn Buddha statue,
     with its long history and spiritual connotation, carries wisdom and faith, endowed by
     BOKING artistThe wonderful dialogue between ancient classic shapes and contemporary
     art makes us feel a kind of beauty beyond the material world.
