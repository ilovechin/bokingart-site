Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Overview

This website presents Bokingart collections and accepts global project inquiries for crystal artworks, sculptural furniture, art lighting, and bespoke installations. The site is designed as a simple static website, and the inquiry form opens the visitor's email app instead of storing form submissions in a database.

Information You May Provide

When you contact Bokingart, you may choose to provide:

  • Name, company, email address, country, and project role.
  • Project type, preferred collection, quantity, schedule, budget range, and design requirements.
  • Images, drawings, floor plans, or other project references that you send by email.

How Information Is Used

Inquiry information is used to respond to your request, recommend suitable product categories, prepare quotations, coordinate project communication, and support after-sales or logistics discussions when applicable.

Sharing

Bokingart may share necessary project information with internal team members, production partners, logistics providers, or professional service providers only when needed to answer or fulfill your request. We do not sell visitor inquiry information.

Cookies And Analytics

This first static version does not require account registration. If analytics, advertising pixels, or embedded third-party tools are added later, this policy should be updated before launch in those markets.

International Communication

Because Bokingart works with global buyers, your inquiry may be handled across countries or regions for quotation, design, production, export, logistics, and customer service purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests related to an inquiry, contact Bokingart at contact@bokingart.com.
