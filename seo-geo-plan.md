# Bokingart SEO / GEO Plan

This document defines the first overseas search and generative engine optimization plan for Bokingart / 铂晶艺术. The goal is to make the brand understandable to Google, Bing, ChatGPT-style answer engines, Perplexity-style research engines, and global buyers looking for high-end art, furniture, lighting, and bespoke crystal projects.

## 1. Core Brand Entities

- Brand English name: Bokingart
- Brand aliases: BOKING, Boking Art, Boking Living, Boking Made, 铂晶艺术
- Founder / lead artist: Dai Jie Wu
- Origin: creative practice beginning in 2001
- Formal company establishment: 2012
- Core material: proprietary synthetic crystal
- Brand base: Huizhou creative cultural park, approximately 30,000 sqm
- Brand idea: Art of Great Purity; Life becomes art, art becomes life
- Main categories: collectible synthetic crystal artworks, sculptural crystal furniture, crystal art lighting, decorative objects, art gifts, bespoke crystal art installations
- Target buyers: global collectors, villa owners, interior designers, architects, hospitality groups, cultural venues, galleries, dealers, procurement teams
