Brand Positioning

Crystal as an artistic language for contemporary Eastern aesthetics.

Bokingart originated from the creative practice behind Shenzhen Yuemu Tang Art Design in 2001 and was formally established as Shenzhen Boking Art Culture Co., Ltd. in 2012. Led by artist Dai Jie Wu, the brand developed a distinctive synthetic crystal material and transformed it into a sculptural medium for art, furniture, lighting, and custom spatial works.

The brand operates through a 30,000 sqm creative cultural park in Huizhou, integrating creation, research, production, and cultural communication for collectors, designers, hospitality groups, and global project owners.

Material & Craft

A proprietary crystal medium shaped by art, research, and hand-finished detail.

Bokingart spent nearly a decade developing a unique synthetic crystal material. Its optical clarity, fluid surface, and sculptural flexibility allow the studio to reinterpret water, Taihu stones, ripples, clouds, and traditional Chinese cultural symbols through contemporary forms.

About Bokingart

Life becomes art. Art becomes life.

Guided by founder and artist Dai Jie Wu, Bokingart brings together designers from leading art academies and a production system capable of translating artistic concepts into collectible works and functional pieces.

Major series include Taihu Stone and Goodness Like Water, while functional works such as crystal tea tables, water-inspired chairs, lounge pieces, and transparent art furniture continue to define a new field of crystal living aesthetics.

Material

Inorganic, non-toxic synthetic crystal with sculptural plasticity, optical clarity, and strong yellowing resistance in catalog testing.

Series

Goodness Like Water, Taihu Stone, Cultural Renaissance, World of Sprite, Statue Beauty, Crystal Legend, Endless Vitality.

Applications

Collectible art, art furniture, art lighting, decorative objects, gifts, villas, hotels, cultural venues, and corporate spaces.

FAQ

Questions global buyers often ask.

What is Bokingart best known for?

Bokingart is known for collectible synthetic crystal artworks, transparent sculptural furniture, art lighting, and bespoke crystal art installations rooted in contemporary Eastern aesthetics.

Who are the ideal clients?

Interior designers, architects, luxury villa owners, hospitality groups, galleries, cultural venues, collectors, and global project buyers seeking a distinctive art-led material language.

Which Bokingart catalogs are represented on this website?

The site structure uses all six supplied Bokingart PDFs: the 2025 brand introduction, 2025 brand catalog, 2025 case catalog, artistic lighting catalog, artistic furniture catalog, and artwork catalog.

Can pieces be made for a specific project?

Yes. The studio can support commissioned pieces, installation direction, dimensions, lighting scenarios, material expression, packaging, and project coordination.
