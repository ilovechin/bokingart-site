    About Boking

    铂晶艺术起源于2001年，艺术家吴代杰的一间艺术工作室，经过十余年的艰苦研究与发展，研究的方向是以自身特有的透明材质为载体进行艺术创作，在2012年正式成立深圳市铂晶艺
    术文化有限公司，思想无疆，BOKING铂晶艺术作品由原创艺术品跨界到艺术家居、艺术灯饰,高级定制等各个领域，拥有完整的艺术家团队、创意设计团队、艺术顾问团队、制作团队。
    曾多次受邀为习主席会客厅等重要场所创作作品。
    新视野、新材质、新工艺给艺术家们带来新的灵感，多年以来创作了铂晶多维度的艺术表现形式，这些纯净璀璨的作品呈现出非常纯粹的个性化形态，是自然、科技、能量和情感的结晶，
    这些探索性的先锋艺术作品充满了美感与生命力，并被分享给世界各地的艺术生活鉴赏家……

.
    BOKING Art was founded in 2001. Under the leadership of the artist Wu Daijie ，BOKING Art has been pursuing its own true colors. The direction of research is to use its own unique crystal
    transparent material as the carrier for artistic creation. Artists are ver y fascinated by this peculiar material. Their persistent spirit makes them constantly break through the limit and repeatedly Create
    a miracle.It currently has more than 100 employees and is a national high-tech enterprise with more than 100 intellectual property rights.
    New horizons, new materials, and new techniques have brought new inspiration to artists. Over the years, they have created multi-dimensional artistic expressions of crystals. These pure
    and bright works present a very pure and personalized form, which is a combination of nature,technology, and energy. The crystallization of emotion and emotion, these exploratory avant-
    garde works of art are full of beauty and vitality, and are shared with connoisseurs of art life all over the world..
