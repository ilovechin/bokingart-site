Artwork Direction

Water, stone, myth, culture, and crystal memory.

The Boking Living artwork catalog organizes the brand's collectible art universe into seven families: Goodness Like Water, Cultural Renaissance, World of Sprite, Charm of Taihu Stone, Beauty of the Statue, Crystal Legend, and Co-Branded IP.

These works translate Chinese cultural imagery and natural forms into transparent sculptural objects with strong optical impact. They are suitable for feature walls, foyer displays, private collections, art corners, hospitality suites, galleries, and landmark cultural projects.

  • Collectible editions and statement objects for art-led interiors.
  • Series language rooted in water, Taihu stones, sprites, statues, and crystal geology.
  • Custom scale, display setting, lighting coordination, and project advisory available by inquiry.
